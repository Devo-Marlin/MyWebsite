Who I Am

Engineering student with a garage habit.

I'm currently studying engineering, but most of what I've actually learned about building things has come from projects outside the classroom — tearing down a Miata, tuning a flight controller, or debugging a Raspberry Pi that refuses to talk to a motor driver. Coursework gives me the theory; the projects are where it gets tested.

What I Build

Cars, drones, and mechatronic systems.

My current work spans a track-focused 1991 Mazda MX-5 build, a custom 5" FPV freestyle drone with a 3D-printed modular frame, a Raspberry Pi and Arduino-driven mechanical launcher (Beerlista), and a collegiate race car project (Formula G). Different domains, same process: design it, build it, test it, and change what doesn't work.

How I Think

Iterate in public. Keep it mechanically simple.

I'd rather ship a simple, reliable version and improve it in documented steps than chase a perfect first attempt. That shows up in the Miata build (mechanical simplicity over complexity) and in the drone (tool-less, modular parts that are easy to replace after a crash).

Photography and cinematography aren't separate from the engineering — they're how the process gets communicated once it's done.

Working Toward

Independent engineering & content, long-term.

The direction I'm building toward is entrepreneurial — combining engineering, fabrication, and media into projects and products of my own rather than just documenting other people's. Everything on this site is a step in that direction.
